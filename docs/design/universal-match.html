Interactive design study · sample scores only. Nothing here changes your saved matches. The colour direction is provisional; this is a layout and usability review, not the finished brand.

What this study is testing

Same game state across phone, tablet and laptop. Large confirmed scores; an active player identified by words and border; a separate unrecorded visit; reachable touch controls; light and dark views; and a larger read-only score display. This sample uses the app’s X01 rules for scoring, including busts and finishes. No accounts, cameras, online rooms or device compatibility are claimed.

Research: current Autodarts · Target OMNI / DartCounter · Scolia.
