# Saved local bull-off — 30 October 2026

v0.11 implements the local bull-off portion of SC-01, through the same replay, persistence, recovery and history contracts as scoring. It is not a camera-detection or online-authority claim.

## Format and capability

Setup explicitly chooses a manual starter or a bull-off between two physical local players, and identifies who throws for the bull first. Inner bull beats outer bull, matching bulls require a rethrow, and two outside-bull darts require a human closer-dart decision or a rethrow when unsure. Rethrows reverse order; a dislodged dart invalidates the round. Only darts remaining in the scoring surface are recorded; bounce-outs are rethrown. These chosen local rules were checked against [GVDA’s dated rules, section 2.6 B](https://www.thegvda.org/paperwork/rulesw2022.pdf); other organiser-specific policies are not claimed.

The app records ring categories and human decisions. It does not infer physical distances, invent coordinates or simulate a physical opponent’s outside-bull dart. Practice therefore uses a chosen starter; bull-off is visibly unavailable for that mode and rejected by the domain validator. This capability boundary remains explicit rather than silently changing the comparison rule.

## Durable state and ownership

A selected bull-off begins inside the saved match, before scoring. Each throw, closer-dart decision or dislodged-round rethrow is a replayable command. Ring ties derive their new round deterministically. Refresh and pause/resume preserve the exact next thrower. Scoring, scoring drafts, leg transitions and automatic bot input cannot bypass an undecided bull-off.

The winner sets the actual initial match starter, including subsequent leg/set starter rotation. The original setup and event log remain unchanged; replay derives the result. Undo/redo can return to an earlier round or decision. Pending bull-off progress is protected by the existing replacement confirmation even when no scoring visits exist. A nearby undo action helps correct the last bull-off event without scrolling to the score controls.

The round log is separate from recorded scoring visits. No bull-off dart contributes to scores, averages, best visits or checkout metrics. Match history and export/import retain the same categories, decisions and throw order. A rematch with a chosen starter receives a new ID, empty command history and no inherited bull-off. To run another bull-off, start a new configured match.

## Compatibility and verification

New sessions use v4. v1–v3 remain supported, while older versions carrying bull-off fields/commands and unsupported future versions fail validation. Import review recomputes the starter rather than trusting an exported result. Session version is preserved through normalisation and backup; outer workspace/backup envelope versions remain unchanged.

Nine domain cases cover order, unequal rings, repeated same-bull ties, outside comparison/tie, dislodged darts, scoring/draft guards, starter rotation, pause/undo/replay, backup and rematch boundaries. Four browser journeys cover refresh/tie/undo, phone comparison/pause/rethrows, protected replacement/export review, and the practice capability boundary.

Fresh Chromium screenshots at 1366 and 390 CSS pixels exercise long player names and outside-dart decisions without horizontal body overflow. The round table scrolls within its own container on small screens.

Local verification passes TypeScript and production build, with 133 unit tests, 50 development-browser tests and five production offline/update tests.

## Remaining gates

Physical player/device usability, richer bull-off rule policies, team representatives, keyboard/screen-reader audit, connected coordinate capabilities and online referee authority remain outstanding. This does not complete the full platform or claim universal provider/rules compatibility.
